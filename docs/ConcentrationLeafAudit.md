# Concentration Leaf Audit

Stage SC-final audits the scalar concentration branch after Markov/Chebyshev,
subGaussian implications, Rademacher/Hoeffding, and subExponential/Bernstein
work. The branch remains experimental and is exposed through
`import HighDimProb.Concentration` and `import HighDimProb.Experimental`.

## Aggregate Import Audit

| Module | Role | Imports audited | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| `HighDimProb/Concentration.lean` | Experimental aggregate for scalar concentration proofs. | Imports every current concentration leaf: `Basic`, `Markov`, `Chebyshev`, `LayerCake`, `OrliczToTail`, `TailToOrlicz`, `Implications`, `MomentImplications`, `MGF`, `MaxScale`, `SubGaussianSums`, `SubExponentialSums`, `Bernstein`, `RademacherSums`, `Hoeffding`. | experimental, complete aggregate |
| `HighDimProb/Scalar.lean` | Stable scalar object aggregate. | Imports scalar object/predicate layer only; does not import concentration theorem leaves. | stable |
| `HighDimProb/Analysis.lean` | Experimental deterministic helper aggregate. | Imports `Analysis.RealInequalities`. | experimental helper |
| `HighDimProb/Distributions.lean` | Experimental concrete distribution aggregate. | Imports `Distributions.Rademacher` and `Distributions.RademacherFamily`, used by Rademacher concentration leaves. | experimental |

No concentration leaf is missing from `HighDimProb.Concentration`.

## Status Notes

- No concentration leaf is obsolete.
- `LayerCake` and `Implications` are aggregate/helper leaves, not theorem owners.
- The stable root `HighDimProb` does not import concentration theorem leaves.
- `HighDimProb.Concentration` should remain experimental until the remaining
  equivalence and raw-predicate gaps are resolved or explicitly accepted.
