## Remaining Non-Proven Families

- Reverse/source MGF implications from tail/Orlicz/moment hypotheses remain
  future work.
- Raw-predicate Bernstein variants remain typed statement scaffolds until a
  raw-to-lintegral bridge or equivalent source assumptions are available.
- Matrix Bernstein, Hanson-Wright, random matrix concentration, WLLN, and SLLN
  remain outside this scalar concentration closure.
